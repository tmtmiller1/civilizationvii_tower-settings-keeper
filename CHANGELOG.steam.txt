==== 1.0.3 | 2026-10-06 | src 26ab48322e95 | gen 986eb26d3562 ====
[b]Version 1.0.3 · 6 October 2026[/b]
• Writes made in the same moment are combined into one engine write (reads in between see them at once), so a mod that saves many pieces in a row no longer stalls the game for the sum of them. Measured with a 530 KB store: sixty consecutive writes went from 0.6 s to one write of about 8 ms.

==== 1.0.1 | 2026-10-06 | src 1563a152cd35 | gen dd62b7fedee8 ====
[b]Version 1.0.1 · 6 October 2026[/b]
• A store whose only remaining entry cannot be named is rebuilt around that entry (its text and slices kept) instead of being left in the fallback layout, so the store is one row and removing the keeper later is safe.
• A kept copy of an unnamed entry moves under its name once a later key list recognises it.
• Stale raw copies hiding behind the root for keys the keeper already holds are removed.

==== 1.0.0 | 2026-10-06 | src 1c42e73fd494 | gen a702c26561c4 ====
[b]Version 1.0.0 · 6 October 2026[/b]
• First release. Every localStorage key kept inside the one row the game reads correctly; modSettings passed through for the options helpers; the store reports one entry; entries another mod pushed ahead of the root are moved into place when they can be named and left untouched when they cannot.
• The root always carries the keeper's mark and is never built on a row without it, so a row another mod writes raw can never be copied over the shared settings.
• A store the keeper could not sort out is re-examined on every launch and brought back to the normal layout once the row in the way can be named.
